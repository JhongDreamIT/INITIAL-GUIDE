How to Join DreamIT ESL

Simple step-by-step guide for International Students

⚠️ CRITICAL RULE ⚠️
All of your documents MUST be sent in PDF format! Do not send pictures, screenshots, or Word documents. They must be PDFs. All papers must also be in English (or translated).
📄

Step 1: Apply to the School

  • Your Passport: A PDF copy valid for at least 6 more months.
  • School Diploma: A PDF copy of your high school diploma or higher.
  • Bank Statement: A PDF copy proving you have funds. Must be less than 60 days old and show at least $15,000 USD (+ $3,500 per family member).
    If someone else is paying for your studies (a sponsor), they must download and fill out this:
    Sponsor Form
  • Fees & Tuition: Pay the non-refundable $100 application fee and registration fees.
✉️

Step 2: Get Your School Papers

  • Your official Acceptance Letter.
  • Your Form I-20 (a super important government document you need to enter the U.S.).
🛂

Step 3: Get Your U.S. Student Visa

  • Pay the SEVIS Fee: Pay the "I-901 fee" online and print the receipt.
  • Book an Interview: Schedule a meeting at the nearest U.S. Embassy/Consulate.
  • Go to Your Interview: Bring your papers, pass the interview, and get your F-1 Visa stamped!
✈️

Step 4: Travel and Start Classes!

  • Fly to the U.S.: Show border officers your passport, visa, and Form I-20.
  • Go to the School: Walk into DreamIT ESL in person before your start date.
  • Take a Test: Take a placement test for the perfect class fit.
  • Finish Paying: Pick classes and pay remaining tuition.
  • Orientation: Attend the welcome meeting and begin!

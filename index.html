How to join DreamIT ESL.

The simple, step-by-step guide to moving from your home country to studying in the U.S. as an International Student.

⚠️ Critical Rule

All of your documents MUST be sent in PDF format! Do not send pictures, phone screenshots, or word documents. They must be PDFs.

🗣️ Also, all of your papers must be written in English (or translated into English). Remember to read the school’s catalog and refund rules before you start.

The Step-by-Step Process

  • Your Passport: A PDF copy of your passport. It must be valid for at least 6 more months.
  • School Diploma: A PDF copy of your high school diploma (or a higher college degree).
  • Bank Statement: A PDF copy of a paper from your bank proving you have enough money to live and study.
    • It must be less than 60 days old.
    • It must show at least $15,000 USD (plus $3,500 for each family member coming with you).
  • Fees & Tuition: Pay the non-refundable $100 application fee, along with any registration and tuition fees required by the school.
  • Acceptance Letter: Your official Acceptance Letter from the school.
  • Form I-20: A super important government document you need to enter the U.S.
  • Pay the SEVIS Fee: Pay the government "I-901 fee" online and print the receipt.
  • Book an Interview: Schedule a meeting at the nearest U.S. Embassy or Consulate in your country.
  • Go to Your Interview: Bring your school papers, pass your interview, and get your F-1 Student Visa stamped inside your passport!
  • Fly to the U.S.: Show the border officers your passport, visa, and Form I-20 to enter the country.
  • Go to the School: Walk into DreamIT ESL in person before the "start date" written on your Form I-20.
  • Take a Test: Take a placement test so the teachers know which English class is the perfect fit for you.
  • Finish Paying & Sign Up: Pick your classes and complete any remaining tuition payments.
  • Go to Orientation: Attend the welcome meeting, get your student handbook, and start your first day of school!
